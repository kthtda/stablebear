14class TimeDelayEmbedding: 

15 """Delay embedding of a time series. 

16 

17 The embedding of a channel ``x`` with ``dimension`` ``m`` and ``delay`` 

18 ``d`` looks back by default: it evaluates at every time ``t`` from 

19 ``t_0 + (m - 1) * d`` to the end of the channel's domain, its last 

20 observation or, for a window (:class:`Window`), the window's end, to the 

21 point ``(x(t - (m - 1) * d), ..., x(t - d), x(t))``. With 

22 ``direction="forward"``, it evaluates at every time ``t`` from ``t_0`` to 

23 the end of the domain minus ``(m - 1) * d``, to the point 

24 ``(x(t), x(t + d), ..., x(t + (m - 1) * d))``. Both are oldest first, so 

25 the forward point at ``t`` is the backward point at ``t + (m - 1) * d``. 

26 Each end is included when the channel's is, and each observation gains a 

27 leading axis of length ``m``. 

28 

29 Its times are the start of its domain and the times in it where a 

30 component reaches an observation, ``t_i + k * d`` for ``k`` from 0 to 

31 ``m - 1``, or ``t_i - k * d`` looking forward, so it has at most ``m`` 

32 times per observation of the channel. Shifted times that differ only by 

33 rounding and give the same point appear once, at the time they snap to: 

34 the channel's own timestamp if one is among them, such as 0.3 rather 

35 than ``0.2 + 0.1``, else the latest of them. The first time is always 

36 the start of the domain. 

37 

38 Each point's components are the channel evaluated at its delayed times, 

39 with the channel's snapping. Between its times the embedding evaluates 

40 by previous value, as any series does, with its own snapping. It is a 

41 view: it shares the series' observations and copies none. 

42 

43 Parameters 

44 ---------- 

45 dimension : int 

46 The number of components ``m``, at least 1. 

47 delay : float, np.timedelta64, or datetime.timedelta 

48 The time between components, positive. A number is in the series' 

49 unit; a duration such as ``np.timedelta64(3, "h")`` is converted to 

50 the unit of each series embedded. Months and years have no fixed 

51 length and are rejected. 

52 direction : {"backward", "forward"}, default "backward" 

53 Whether the components look back from ``t`` or ahead of it. 

54 

55 Raises 

56 ------ 

57 ValueError 

58 On an invalid ``dimension``, ``delay``, or ``direction``, or when a 

59 channel spans less than ``(m - 1) * d``. 

60 """
